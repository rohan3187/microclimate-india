Zooming in into the secrets of Myristica swamps

microclimateindia · Oct 16, 2024 · 1 min read

Myristica swamps are the unique ecosystems found across the Western Ghats of India. They occur in the valleys where streams originate and flow throughout the year.

Read more about this project

Find out how we are monitoring moisture and diversity in the swamps near Sirsi and Shimoga on our Myristica swamps project page.
