Data Portal · Coming soon

Open microclimate data for the Western Ghats

The project aims to share, collaborate and provide data for research. We are building a portal where anyone can explore, visualise and download the records from our probe network.

What's coming

Planned portal features

Explore by probe

Pick a probe on the map and see its full record, habitat and history.

Interactive charts

Daily, monthly and seasonal temperature and soil moisture, compared across sites.

Download

Cleaned, quality-checked data as CSV, with metadata for every probe.

Summaries

Buffering indices, extremes and dry-season statistics, ready for ecological analyses.

What the data looks like

Every probe produces one row every 15 minutes, which adds up to about 35,000 rows per probe per year.

FieldDescription
probe_idUnique probe code (see the probe map)
datetimeTimestamp, 15-minute interval
t_soilSoil temperature at −6 cm (°C)
t_surfaceSurface temperature at +2 cm (°C)
t_airAir temperature at +15 cm (°C)
soil_moistureSoil moisture (raw counts and volumetric estimate)
qc_flagQuality-control flag

Data use & citation

Data from the Microclimate India network is shared for non-commercial research and conservation. Please acknowledge the Microclimate India Research (MIR) project and its collaborators in any publication. We welcome co-authorship discussions where our data forms a substantial part of the work.

Help fund the portal

Hosting, cleaning and publishing years of 15-minute records takes time and infrastructure. A donation helps us make this data freely available sooner.
