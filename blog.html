Microclimatic niche separation among swamp, forest, and edge species in Myristica swamps of the Western Ghats, India

Balachandra Hegde, Narasimha Hegde, H. V. Raghavendra, Rohan Shetti, Yogendra Kambalagere

Wetlands Ecology and Management 34(2), article 30 · Published online 12 April 2026

This study uses microclimate loggers placed along swamp, edge and forest transects to examine how plant species of Myristica swamps, their edges and the surrounding evergreen forest occupy different microclimatic niches. It is part of our Myristica swamps project.
