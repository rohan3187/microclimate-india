Why your support matters

India's first microclimate network, and it runs on your support

Most tropical biodiversity lives under the canopy of large forests. Plants, frogs, insects, fungi and seedlings there experience a climate of their own: cooler, wetter and more stable than the weather outside. This microclimate decides which species survive as the planet warms, yet it is almost entirely neglected in research and conservation planning.

No microclimate data exists for India. Weather stations measure open ground, not the forest floor. Our network of 34 loggers across the Western Ghats is a pioneering effort to fill that gap. Every year of records is a baseline that cannot be collected later.

The project is not funded by any institution. It is driven by a group of scientists and supports many local researchers, students and landholders in the Western Ghats. Your gift keeps the loggers recording, funds the field work, and builds the open dataset that conservation in India needs.

  • Pioneering science: the first long-term microclimate records from Indian forests.
  • Local researchers: funds field work and training for researchers from the region.
  • Direct impact: funds go to instruments, field work and data, not overheads.
  • Long-term value: each probe records for up to ~10 years, so one gift keeps producing data for a decade.
  • Open science: the data will be shared freely through our upcoming portal.

Adopt a probe

Put your name on the map, and in the data

Adopting a probe is the most direct way to grow the network. Each probe records soil, surface and air temperature and soil moisture every 15 minutes, for years.

As a probe adopter you receive:

  • Your name (or a dedication) on the probe's pin on our interactive probe map
  • Photos and the location of "your" probe when it is installed
  • A yearly summary of what your probe recorded
  • Acknowledgement on our website and in project reports

Ideal as a gift, a memorial, a school or college class project, or a company team initiative.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Will I receive a receipt?

Yes. Write to us at microclimateindia@gmail.com and we will send an acknowledgement and receipt for every contribution.

Can I donate from outside India?

Please contact us first. We will advise the right route for international contributions.

Can I visit a probe site?

Sometimes. Many sites are in protected or private forest, but we organise occasional field visits for supporters. Let us know if you are interested.

How do I know my money was well used?

Probe adopters receive yearly updates. All supporters can follow progress on the probe map, the blog and our annual note on spending.
